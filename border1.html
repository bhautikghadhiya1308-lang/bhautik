Once there was a shepherd in a village. He used to take his flock of sheep to the nearby forests for grazing. After a few days, he found his job boring and thought of making it more fun. One fine day, while the sheep were grazing, he gave a loud cry, “Wolf!, Wolf! Somebody, please help. A wolf is taking away my lamb.” The farmers working closeby heard the cry and came running for its rescue. Upon reaching, they could not find any wolves. The boy jokingly said that he lied just for fun. After realising this, people left the place. But the boy kept on playing similar tricks frequently. One day the boy really cried for help when the wolf arrived and attacked the flock. But this time, nobody believed him and thought he was again trying to fool them.
